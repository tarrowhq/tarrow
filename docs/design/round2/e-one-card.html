E — One Card

The opposite bet from D. Not "draw more" — show less at once. Every version so far has been a scrolling document, which means the reader scrolls to find out whether the news is bad, and disclosure sits below the fold where rule 5 says it was never delivered.

Here the finding gets one entire screen and nothing else — no masthead, no nav, no footer in view. Everything else becomes a stack of full-screen cards, each carrying exactly one idea: one school, one gap, one action. Nothing is hidden; only one thing is ever competing for attention.

Mechanism: CSS scroll-snap. No script, no JS-dependent state. With scripting off it degrades to a long page with big sections — the no-JS requirement satisfied by construction rather than by a fallback. Commits to one dark world in every viewer theme: a phone at night, on a library machine, is the case this is built for.

Why this happens

Three likely reasons

The address is not in Summit County · the county writes it differently (road type, direction, unit number) · or the county has not published a point for it.

tarrow will not fall back to the middle of the street or the centre of the ZIP code. A confident answer about the wrong building is more dangerous than no answer.
