## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
The data contract: jurisdiction and level, buffer distance, facility classes, measurement method, effective date, citation with resolvable URL, retrieved-on, verified-on, verifier. Facility classes are a controlled vocabulary shared with the POI pipeline, not free text. Include applicability fields (offense-date predicates) now even though nothing evaluates them yet — Ohio retroactivity is settled law (Hyle v. Porter, 2008; schools from 2003-07-31, preschool and child care from 2007-07-01), so the engine will need them, and reserving fields costs a few lines while retrofitting them costs a migration across every authored rule. Provenance fields are required, not optional: a field that is not required cannot be gated on.
