## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Make the boundaries of what somap knows legible to someone under stress who is not a lawyer.

Users often arrive on a deadline, sometimes a thirty-day order to move. Disclosure written for legal cover rather than comprehension fails them and fails Principle I, because a limitation nobody understands protects nobody.

Covers the standing limitations: signage and zoning qualification, source completeness gaps, staleness, the private right of action, and helper-not-authority framing. Each appears where it is relevant to a result, not collected in a terms page nobody reads.

Confirming with the registering sheriff office is the recommended action throughout, so this should name the right office for the searched address and make contacting it easy.
